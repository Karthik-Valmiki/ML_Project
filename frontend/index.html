Methodological design

Cross-validation uses stratified 5-fold partitions to maintain identical normal-to-attack ratios across all iterations. Balanced bootstrapping is applied strictly to training folds to address minority attack representation; holdout test partitions remain untouched to reflect operational network conditions.

Principal component analysis

Projection of 61 dimensions onto orthogonal axes. The first two components capture 67.3% of cumulative flow variance, separating high-velocity flooding from benign sessions in 2D space without label leakage.

Unsupervised K-means clustering

Inertia elbow analysis establishes optimal partitioning at K=3. Discovered clusters separate traffic into three profiles: standard web browsing, volumetric DoS streams, and low-rate port probing.
